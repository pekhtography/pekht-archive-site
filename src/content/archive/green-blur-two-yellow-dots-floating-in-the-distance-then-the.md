Green blur. Two yellow dots floating in the distance.
Then the branch—
lilac bells, each one (a small cup, a soft mouth, a closed eye)
tilted toward the ground.🌸
The more it blooms, the deeper it bows.🌿
This one branch is every full life: too heavy to stand straight.
