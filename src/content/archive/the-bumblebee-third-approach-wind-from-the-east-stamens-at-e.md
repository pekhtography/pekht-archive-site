The bumblebee
(third approach, wind from the east, stamens at eleven o'clock)
ran the full intake-to-effort ratio above the yellow trumpet🌼
and hovered—
wings grinding silk into noise—
then filed the flower under 'revisit Tuesday.'🐝
The daylily bloomed once. That was Tuesday.
