The river glitters slow and golden,
a tugboat coughs out a puff of diesel—
and then this giant concrete spaceship appears,
hovering on chopsticks,
deciding whether to sit down or launch back into orbit.
It stayed.
Now the flowers are its landing pad🌊🛸🌸
