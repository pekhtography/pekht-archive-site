Cold grips the air.
Gold towers erupt—park got promoted to palace,
forgot to tell the benches👑
Extravagance dropped on simplicity
like a fur coat on a snowman.
Still, light pools warm on frozen paths.
The absurdity sticks.
That's winter's real gift❄️✨
