They rise from the dirt, looking like alien fingers — peonies don’t grow; they perform an entrance

These fluffballs may look cute now, but just wait — soon they’ll throw a full-blown floral fashion show

One bead of water on a pale petal. 💧 
The whole garden asleep, the whole sky pressing down. 
And this one bud — tight, cool, glowing faintly pink. 🌸 
Storing everything.

Sun hits. Petals unfurl. 
The creased, sugary, velvet layers (thicker than a wedding cake) 
expand until the backyard becomes a cheap motel. 
Royal arrival. 🍭🏰

The pink crowd already forgot her. 
She dried up above them, rattling — 
a handful of seeds, 
the weight of a whole summer pressed into one papery whisper 🌿🤍🌸
