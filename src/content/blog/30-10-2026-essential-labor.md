With wings stretched wide, the Cinereous Vulture crafts poetry in the sky, a master of majestic solitude.

The vulture may be seen as ominous, yet it plays a vital role in the cycle of life.

Cold wind rakes the feathers flat. Curved beak, surgeon-grade. Cleans what the world leaves, asks nothing, bows to no one. The work is the dignity.

Looks like the villain. Sounds like gravel scraping stone in the dark. Sits completely still while everything else panics. Nature's most essential employee has the worst PR team.
