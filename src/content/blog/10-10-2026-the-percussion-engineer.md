Nature’s ultimate specialist. Doesn’t get headaches, just gives them out. A masterclass in single-minded, wood-pulverizing focus.

Mossy bark. Pale light. Sharp angles. One beak strikes. Hard. Fast. You call it a headache; he calls it a three-bedroom apartment with a view. Rent-free.

Red flash on grey bark. Twenty strikes per second — skull engineered for exactly this. The rest of us buy stress balls.

Dress code: tuxedo and red beret. Venue: the snow floor. Menu: sunflower seeds. The forest's chief percussion engineer — tonight performing live as a sparrow. Formal feathers. Budget dinner.

No tree. No drumroll. Red-capped silence, beak in the January ground — the kind of quiet only something built for noise can make. Built for percussion. Chose the pause.
