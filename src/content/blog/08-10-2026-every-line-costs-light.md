I watched Frost paint me on the window, as if I never left the sky. He draws love best when it’s already slipping away.

I fade, and Frost keeps drawing, turning absence into pattern, silence into beauty. Some devotion survives only on cold glass.

The window becomes a canvas. Frost moves fast, stealing color from the sinking sky; art begins where light starts running out.

Every line costs light. Each pattern burns a little more sky. The masterpiece grows while the source disappears.

The light is gone. Only the patterns remain, flawless, cold, complete. Art survives. The color never does.
