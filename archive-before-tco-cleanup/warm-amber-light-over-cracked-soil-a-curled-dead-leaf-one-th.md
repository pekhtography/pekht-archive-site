Warm amber light over cracked soil, a curled dead leaf,
one thin weed pushing through—
and then, at the center, warm-brown and mottled,
smelling faintly of nest and earth🌿,
this half-feathered thing standing still
as every unfinished creature that ever bet on itself🐦
