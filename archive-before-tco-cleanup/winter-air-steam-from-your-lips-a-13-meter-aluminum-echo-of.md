Winter air. Steam from your lips. 
A 13-meter aluminum echo of a soft, unfinished thought. 
The city lights turn its cold metal into a warm, 
buttery memory of hands in clay. 
It’s not a monument to an idea. 
It’s a monument to the hesitation before the idea. 🖐️🔥🧊
